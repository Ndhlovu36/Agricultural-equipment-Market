Privacy Policy - PiOS App - Gauteng, South Africa
agricultural-equipment-market.vercel.app | Last Updated: 4 September 2026

1. Introduction

Agricultural Equipment Market ("we", "us") operates agricultural-equipment-market.vercel.app, a PiOS utility app for buying and selling agricultural equipment, tractors, planters, spares and trailers in South Africa. We use Pi Network authentication and Pi payments only. We respect your privacy.

2. Information We Collect

a) Pi Network Information: When you Login with Pi, we receive your Pi username and UID provided by Pi Network SDK for authentication.

b) Transaction Information: When you make a Pi payment for tractors, equipment or spares, we receive payment ID, amount (in Pi), and transaction ID from Pi Blockchain to confirm your order.

c) Order Information: Equipment type, quantity, delivery location (city/town) you enter for delivery purposes.

We do NOT collect email, phone number, passwords, or payment card data. We do NOT access Pi wallet private keys.

3. How We Use Information

To authenticate you via Pi, process Pi payments via Pi Blockchain, fulfill your equipment orders, provide customer support, and improve our app.

4. Pi Network SDK

Our app uses Pi SDK (Pi.init, Pi.authenticate, Pi.createPayment). All payments are processed securely on Pi Blockchain. We never see or store your Pi passphrase.

5. Data Sharing

We do not sell your data. We only share payment ID with Pi API (api.minepi.com) to approve and complete payments as required by Pi Platform. Delivery partners may receive city/location to deliver equipment.

6. Data Retention

We retain order records for 3 years for warranty and accounting as required by South African law. Pi username retained while your account is active.

7. Your Rights (POPIA)

Under South Africa POPIA Act, you have right to access, correct, or delete your data. Contact us to request.

8. Security

We use HTTPS, Vercel secure hosting, and Pi Blockchain secure payments. 100% Pi payment - no card storage.

9. Children's Privacy

Not intended for children under 18. We do not knowingly collect data from children.
